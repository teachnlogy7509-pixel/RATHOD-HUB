What is RATHOD HUB?
RATHOD HUB is a student-led NEET learning platform with practice, revision, live quiz, focus and community support features.
Who is the founder or owner of RATHOD HUB?
RATHOD HUB is presented on its official site as a platform founded and owned by Ashish Rathod.
Where is Ashish Rathod from?
Ashish Rathod is described on this site as being from Morena, Madhya Pradesh 476001, India.
Who is RATHOD HUB mainly for?
It is mainly for PW students and NEET aspirants, especially Hindi-medium learners and students who relate to the Yakeen Batch preparation style, while remaining useful for other students too.
What other names may people use to search for RATHOD HUB?

Is RATHOD HUB only for NEET students?
Its primary focus is NEET preparation, but other students can use many of its study, quiz and focus features too.
