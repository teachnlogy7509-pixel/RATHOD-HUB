About RATHOD HUB

RATHOD HUB is an independent NEET preparation platform built for focused study, NCERT-oriented practice, live quiz participation and student learning support. It brings together practice, revision, timer-based study and community features in one place.

What RATHOD HUB is

  • A study-focused platform for NEET aspirants.
  • A place for MCQ practice, PYQ-style revision, live quiz participation, focus study tracking and peer learning.
  • A platform that includes AI Doubt, AI Tutor Pro, AI Learning, Daily 9 PM Battle, NEET 720 tests, Study Material, Focus Timer, Study Rooms and Community tools.
  • An installable web-based learning app experience that runs in modern browsers and supported mobile environments.

What RATHOD HUB is not

  • It is not an official government exam body website.
  • It is not affiliated with NTA or NCERT; it is an independent learning platform built for preparation support.
  • It is not only one single quiz page; it includes multiple study, revision, focus and community modules.

Core features

Live practice

Live Quiz, Daily 9 PM Battle, 45Q Tests, Knowledge Battle, Treasure Hunt and leaderboard-based participation.

Study support

Study Material, AI Notes & Formula, Daily Formula, Focus Timer, Study Diary and My Vault.

Community

Study Rooms, Community, Live Chat, Stories and peer learning features designed around regular preparation.

Founder

Ashish Rathod is presented in the platform as the founder behind RATHOD HUB. This public info page also states that Ashish is connected with PW and handles or supports PW app operations alongside building and managing the RATHOD HUB experience.

Purpose

RATHOD HUB is designed to help students study with focus, practice with discipline and revise with clarity. The goal is to keep preparation tools in one place instead of spreading them across many separate apps and sites.
