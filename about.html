About RATHOD HUB Learning

RATHOD HUB Learning is an independent practice and revision platform built for PW students and focused learners. It brings together quiz participation, focus tools, study support and community-based learning in one place.

What RATHOD HUB Learning is

  • A study-focused platform for PW students and serious learners.
  • A place for MCQ practice, PYQ-style revision, live quiz participation, focus study tracking and smart learning support.
  • A platform that includes AI Doubt, AI Tutor Pro, AI Learning, Daily 9 PM Battle, Study Material, Focus Timer, Study Rooms and Community tools.
  • An installable web-based learning app experience that runs in modern browsers and supported mobile environments.

What RATHOD HUB Learning is not

  • It is not an official government exam body website.
  • It is not the official website of PW, NTA or NCERT; it is an independent learning support platform.
  • It is not only one single quiz page; it includes multiple study, revision, focus and community modules.

Core features

Live practice

Live Quiz, Daily 9 PM Battle, 45Q Tests, Knowledge Battle, Treasure Hunt and leaderboard-based participation.

Study support

Study Material, AI Notes & Formula, Daily Formula, Focus Timer, Study Diary and My Vault.

Community

Study Rooms, Community, Live Chat, Stories and peer learning features designed around regular preparation.

Founder

Ashish Rathod is presented as the founder behind RATHOD HUB Learning. Public info on this site also states a connection with PW while keeping RATHOD HUB as an independent platform.

Purpose

RATHOD HUB Learning is designed to help students study with focus, practice with discipline and revise with clarity. The goal is to keep useful learning tools in one place instead of spreading them across many separate apps and sites.
